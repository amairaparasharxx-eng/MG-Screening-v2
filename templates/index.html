MG Risk & Symptom Questionnaire

This questionnaire gathers information about diagnosis history, family history, and common Myasthenia Gravis (MG) symptoms. It is a screening prototype, not a diagnostic tool.
Disclaimer: This tool is for informational and educational purposes only and does not diagnose MG, Thymoma, or any other condition. The symptoms below can also occur in many other conditions. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about new, persistent, or worsening symptoms, and before starting, stopping, or changing any medication.

Factors Associated With Worsening Symptoms

Research-supported factors to consider: heat or temperature extremes, physical or emotional stress, physical exertion/prolonged muscle use, infection or illness, lack of sleep, pain, surgery or anaesthesia, injury/trauma, menstrual periods (if applicable), pregnancy/postpartum changes (if applicable), thyroid problems, and recent medication changes or missed/withdrawn treatment.

These factors have been reported in MG literature as possible precipitants of clinical worsening or myasthenic crisis. This question records your experience and does not establish that any factor caused your symptoms.
